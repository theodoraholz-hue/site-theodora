Minha pesquisa conecta marketing, inteligência artificial, sistemas de recomendação, inteligência territorial e uso estratégico de dados. Tenho experiência em pesquisa, análise de dados, produção acadêmica e apoio ao ensino superior.

About me

I am a PhD student in Business Administration at the Federal University of Rio Grande do Sul (UFRGS), with a Master's and a Bachelor's degree in Business Administration from the Federal University of Rio Grande (FURG).

My research connects marketing, artificial intelligence, recommendation systems, territorial intelligence and the strategic use of data. I have experience in research, data analysis, academic production and higher education support.
